Frequently asked questions

Here are some our frequently asked questions

Is my CredPal account secure?

Yes, your CredPal account is secured with bank-level encryption and multi-factor authentication to protect your personal
and financial information.

Can I get another spending limit with an active repayment tenure?

Your spending limit may be reviewed based on your repayment history and account activity. Contact support for more
information about limit increases.

What is my Bank Verification Number (BVN) used for?

Your BVN is used to verify your identity and ensure secure account setup in compliance with banking regulations. It helps us
protect your account from fraud.

What can I redeem rewards points for?

Rewards points can be redeemed for discounts on purchases, cashback, or special offers from partner merchants. Check
your rewards dashboard for available options.

What rewards does CredPal's Buy Now Pay Later offer?

CredPal's Buy Now Pay Later offers cashback rewards, early payment discounts, and access to exclusive deals from partner
stores when you make timely payments.

How do I pay my credit balance?

You can pay your credit balance through the CredPal app using bank transfer, debit card, or automatic bank deductions.
Payments can be made in installments or full.
